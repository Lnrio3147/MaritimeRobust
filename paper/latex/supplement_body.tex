Every number in this draft is produced by code in \texttt{scripts/} from artefacts hashed in \texttt{reports/snapshot.json} (230 runs, code, configs, splits, reports). The probes of §4.5 are \texttt{reports/SATURATION\_PROBE.md} (pixel statistics), \texttt{reports/hdr\_render\_probe.json} (soft-shoulder re-render, script \texttt{scripts/hdr\_render\_probe.py}) and \texttt{reports/M1\_KILL\_TEST.md} (mask falsification, script \texttt{scripts/m1\_eval.py}, per-image predictions in \texttt{reports/m1\_preds\_s{17,29}.npz}). The second renderer of §8.4 is \texttt{reports/PHYSICAL\_RENDERER.md}, produced by \texttt{scripts/physical\_eval.py} from \texttt{src/maritime\_robust/physical.py} and gated by \texttt{scripts/physical\_gate.py}; its pre-registration is \texttt{reports/PREREG\_PHYSICAL\_RENDERER.md}. A full re-evaluation of 12 runs reproduced the published R1 statistic bit-exactly (max absolute difference 0). The pre-registration for the severity 2/4 extension is in \texttt{reports/PREREG\_SEVERITY\_2\_4.md}; the analysis and its comparison against the registered predictions is in \texttt{reports/SEVERITY\_CURVE.md}. The error-overlap decomposition of §4.6 is \texttt{reports/COMPOSITION.md} §2, from \texttt{scripts/overlap\_main\_grid.py}; the fifteen-pair composition study and its replication gate are the rest of \texttt{reports/COMPOSITION.md}, from \texttt{scripts/composition\_{calibrate,pairs,interaction,predictors,aggregate,gate}.py}, with the gate pre-registered in \texttt{reports/PREREG\_COMPOSITION\_REPLICATION.md}. \section{Appendix B. Margin-space superadditivity}
The original analysis measured superadditivity in margin space (difference between compound accuracy and the additive expectation of margin losses): haze+glare extra margin loss $-$0.152 … $-$0.032, haze+cloud $-$0.144 … +0.009, across four backbone families. It agrees in sign with the accuracy-space result reported in §4.3 and is not used for inference here.
